Geng Li

Exercise and Brain Science

I am a researcher at the School of Psychology and the Research Center for Exercise and Brain Science, Shanghai University of Sport.

My research examines how physical exercise, sport expertise, and cognitive training shape cognition and the brain. I use behavioral experiments, neuroimaging, and meta-analysis to study executive function, creativity, brain plasticity, and healthy aging.

Research

My current research interests include:

  • Physical exercise, executive function, and brain health
  • Neuroimaging meta-analysis and evidence synthesis
  • Cognitive training, transfer effects, and neural plasticity
  • Sport expertise, creativity, and healthy aging
